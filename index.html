🔒 Your world stays on your device

LostShulker reads only the Minecraft world folder you choose, using your browser's read-only folder permission. The search runs locally in this tab. LostShulker does not upload your save files or scan results to a server.

When the page loads, your browser downloads the site and its NBT scanning library from jsDelivr (and may fetch web fonts from Google). Once everything has finished loading, you can disconnect from the internet and scan in this tab. Refreshing or reopening the page offline may not work.

Not sure where your Minecraft world is? Show me

When the folder picker opens, find the saves folder for your Minecraft installation. Then open the folder for the world you want to search.

Important: Select the individual world folder — the one that contains level.dat and a region folder. Do not select the whole .minecraft folder or the saves folder itself.

Windows (Java Edition)

Press Windows + R, paste %appdata%\.minecraft\saves, and press Enter. Open your world folder there.

macOS (Java Edition)

In Finder, press Shift + Command + G, enter ~/Library/Application Support/minecraft/saves, and press Return. Open your world folder.

Linux (Java Edition)

Open ~/.minecraft/saves, then open your world folder.

Using a launcher or custom game directory?

For Prism Launcher, CurseForge, Modrinth or another launcher, open that instance's game directory, find its saves folder, and choose the individual world folder inside it.

Your browser will ask you to approve access to the folder you choose. LostShulker requests read-only access; you can cancel the picker at any time.

Choose the folder for one world, not the parent saves folder. You can expand the guide above if you need help finding it.
